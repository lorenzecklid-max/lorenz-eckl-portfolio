Solo Project 2026

The Problem

Conventional trailers require storage, preparation and a separate attachment process. This makes them impractical for spontaneous everyday transport. There had to be a better way.

The Idea

I wanted to combine a rear carrier and a trailer in one structure.

So I started planning ...

The main challenge was to figure out the folding mechanism.
The Frame is made of lightweight alluminium for easy transport.
Thorough limit testing to find the right balance between weight and stability.

The Result

The Result is an everyday product, that doubles as a gepäcktrager and a trailor while remaining light and visually restrained.

When not in use, it follows the silhouette of the bicycle and remains visually unobtrusive. When deployed, the structure extends behind the bicycle and functions as a narrow single-wheel trailer.
